About Pixel Verdict

Pixel Verdict is an independent video game review publication dedicated to honest, thoughtful analysis of the games that matter. We believe that a great review does more than assign a number — it articulates why a game resonates, what it achieves, where it stumbles, and who it is for.

Our Review Philosophy

Every game on this site is played to completion — or as close to it as the experience demands. We spend meaningful time with each title before putting a single word on the page. Side quests, multiplayer modes, difficulty options, and post-launch patches all factor into our final scores. We do not accept review copies in exchange for positive coverage; all scores reflect our genuine assessment.

We approach each game on its own terms. A tight six-hour narrative experience is not judged by the same yardstick as a hundred-hour open-world epic. Context — genre conventions, development budget, and stated ambitions — always informs our critique.

Scoring System

Our scores run from 1.0 to 10.0. Here is what each band means:

9–10
Masterpiece. Essential playing for anyone who cares about games as a medium.
8–8.9
Excellent. Minor flaws that don't detract from an outstanding experience.
6–7.9
Good to Decent. Solid and enjoyable, but held back by noticeable issues.
Below 6
Mediocre or Poor. Significant problems that temper or ruin the experience.

What We Cover

Our focus is on major releases across PC, PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo platforms. We prioritise games with significant cultural footprint or critical interest — titles that drive conversation in the broader gaming community. We also periodically revisit older games with retrospective reviews when a significant update, sequel, or anniversary brings renewed attention.

Independence

Pixel Verdict is reader-supported. We have no financial relationship with any publisher, developer, or platform holder. Our scores are never adjusted based on advertising, access, or relationships. If a game is bad, we say so — regardless of how anticipated or well-funded it was.
